THE TOP WALLHOPS

Rules

Verification, completion and list-worthiness requirements.

Rules by WuduTeHg

Verifications

02 rules
  1. The entire verification must be recorded at 60 FPS.
  2. For top 10 verifications, provide at least one of these: a visible keyboard layout, clearly audible keyboard sounds, unedited footage, or MicroProfiler.

Completions

05 rules
  1. For a completion role, submit your completion video in the videos channel or send it to an administrator.
  2. A top 5 completion needs at least one of: a keyboard layout (not a handcam), keyboard sounds, or unedited footage.
  3. Completions must be legitimate and recorded at 60 FPS. Alignment keys are not permitted when they substantially alter the difficulty.
  4. If a glitch lets you bypass part of the course, or makes you miss the final jump, ask the community whether the completion is valid.
  5. Missing the final walk to the win pad does not invalidate an otherwise completed run.

List Worthiness

05 rules
  1. The gameplay must be longer than 3 seconds.
  2. Video evidence of verification is required. Simply spectating the verifier does not qualify.
  3. Wallhops must be the primary source of difficulty, rather than exceptionally difficult ordinary jumps.
  4. Breaks and checkpoints are allowed, but there should not be too many.
  5. Repetitive wallhop gameplay qualifies only when it has excellent decoration (A+ quality) or is notably unique.
Note: After an entry joins the list, its gameplay must stay unchanged or it may be removed. The community decides how to handle minor changes.
